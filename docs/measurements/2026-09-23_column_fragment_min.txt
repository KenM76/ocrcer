Narrowed rule (both sides lone)

WHAT THIS IS
  Readings taken per ARCHITECTURE.md section 11's 2026-09-23 entry "The
  two-component rule failed its gate on real filings; narrowed to 'no cut
  between two lone glyphs'", which supersedes the entry the readings above
  were taken against. `split_at_column_gaps`
  (crates/ocrcer-core/src/layout/lines.rs) now drops a candidate boundary
  only when the raw fragments on *both* sides of it -- as formed by all
  candidates together -- are each exactly one component; a candidate with a
  multi-component fragment on either side is kept. No parameter change; the
  model file is unchanged.

  Also changed to fit the narrowed rule (per the entry's "Decision" list):
  layout::words::a_single_wide_gap_still_separates_two_words moved its
  geometry from `[0, 10, 40]` to `[0, 10, 34]` (the wide gap is now 16 px,
  under the 17.5 px cut at 1.75x10, so the column rule does not fire on it at
  all); its assertions (fallback source, two words, one-member second word)
  are unchanged. layout::lines::a_lone_glyph_between_wide_gaps_does_not_
  fracture_its_band, written for the withdrawn two-component rule, is
  replaced by layout::lines::a_run_of_lone_glyphs_across_wide_gaps_stays_
  one_band and layout::lines::a_lone_glyph_beside_a_multi_glyph_fragment_
  is_still_cut.
