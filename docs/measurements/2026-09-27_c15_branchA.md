## Verdict: branch A fails the gate

On the 160 unseen pages, classifier 1 (branch-A weights, scale 2√2 fitted on
fold A) is **+0.702 pp end-to-end CER and +1.267 pp line-matched CER worse
than classifier 0.** EPS is 0.02 pp. Val, ident and timing_ab were not run.
About 92% of the end-to-end gap is a single substitution, `":" → "±"`, which
does not occur on crops aligned to truth boxes (finding 4). The dense-row
oversampling was too small to matter: 132 rows, 22 unique crops, 0.48% of
real_train (finding 1). On dense lines both classifiers read under 30% of
aligned crops (finding 3).

Deviations from the brief, all disclosed below:
- The dump binary is master's post-`c15-dumpfix` version, so nn15c also drops
  13,833 lattice-mismatch rows that nn15b kept (finding 2).
- Training ran on XPU and is not bit-reproducible.
- The 10 GB VRAM cap was enforced, but peak use was 92 MiB, so it was never
  tested.
- The `bench/ident` corpus was not found on disk and could not be stem-checked.
- `gen15_probe.rs` gained a dense bucket.

Never touches `finfilings`, `finfilings-val`, `pages-cov`, any fixture, or

## Step 1 — row-selection criterion (measured on fold A, before any dump)

**Numeric share** (authored definition): per truth line, ASCII-digit
characters divided by all characters across that line's truth tokens.

**Method.** Added `nn15-dump dense-stats <model.ocrw> <pages-train-dir>
<split-file> <stride>` to `nn15_dump.rs` — a read-only diagnostic that
restricts to fold-A stems (`bench/splits/nn15_page_split.tsv`, `fold == "A"`,
54 stems) and replicates `dump_real_and_nonpath`'s own decode/mask/bands
reconstruction (`page::from_pgm` → `engine.recognize_lines` →
`binarize::binarize_with` → `deskew` → re-binarize → `underline::strip_underlines`
→ `lines::group_with_bands` → `words::split_band_with`), the same public
calls in the same order, satisfying project rule 4 (never a second
segmentation implementation). It stops short of the per-word
crop/feature/matcher work `dump_real_and_nonpath` does, since step 1 only
needs `TextLine.x_height` and each qualifying line's numeric share. For each
qualifying line (truth/decoded word-count match, same gate as the real dump)
it prints `x_height<TAB>numeric_share` to stdout.

criterion for branch A's oversampling.

**Oversampling factor: 6 additional copies per qualifying character crop**
(authored guess, not fitted — there is no principled way to pick this
number ahead of a training run; 6 was chosen as a bounded middle value,
enough to give this ~4% line-level cluster materially more training
signal without letting it dominate gradient updates over the rest of the
corpus). Labelled explicitly as a guess in `docs/PLAN.md`/`ARCHITECTURE.md`
terms; if branch A proceeds to a second iteration this is the first knob to
retune.

**No synthetic rows (decision, not a measurement):** every added
`dense_train` row is an exact duplicate of an already-real, correctly
labelled, non-degenerate crop produced by the same `segment::crop` +
`Glyph::input`/`extract_with_grid` call that wrote that crop's original
single entry to `real_train` — no pixel noise, no jitter, no synthetic
composition. Oversampling here means duplication of real data, never
generation of new data.

**Finding 3 — dense crops are unreadable to both classifiers (measured).** On
dense small-font numeric lines, fewer than 1 in 3 crops that exactly match a
truth box are classified correctly, by either classifier. On unseen pages
the matcher beats the net by about 5 pp there. Branch A moved the net by
+0.8 pp on this bucket (n=249, within noise). The problem on these lines is
the crop (small x-height, the segmentation there), not the classifier's
training data. More training rows for the net cannot fix that.

**Finding 4 — the `":" → "±"` loss (step 5) is not a per-crop error
(measured).** `":"` does not appear among either net's top per-crop
confusions on any sample. So the 2,460 end-to-end colon→± substitutions come
from lattice paths whose crop does not match a truth box, most likely a
colon merged with a neighbour or read across a rule line, which the net
scores confidently as `±` and the matcher does not. This is the same decoder-
interaction failure `2026-09-26_c15_generalisation.md` identified, now pinned
to one concrete symbol pair.
