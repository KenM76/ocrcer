2026-09-22 — First third-party measurement: scribeocr/ocr-benchmark, 29 pages

WHAT THIS IS
  Every accuracy figure elsewhere in this repository is OCRcer scored by
  OCRcer, on truth this project converted, by a metric this project chose.
  This run is none of those: their pages, their truth, their metric, their
  code. `github.com/scribeocr/ocr-benchmark`, cloned to the excluded-private
  tree; no part of it enters the model or the repository.

HOW OCRcer WAS PRESENTED TO IT
  A new `hocr` bin in ocrcer-bench (behind --features pages) writes hOCR with
  ocr_line / ocrx_word elements, bounding boxes, and a baseline on every line.
  The baseline is mandatory: their importer does `if (!baselineMatch) return ''`
  and silently drops any line that lacks one, which presents as `missed = total`
  with `extra = 0`. The two baseline numbers are slope and offset, and the
  offset is relative to the BOX BOTTOM, not the page.

STEP 1 — VALIDATE THE HARNESS ON THE ENGINES NOBODY HERE TUNED
  node run_eval.js over the 29 pages, ignorePunct = true:

    engine                 pooled    mean-of-pages    published
    Tesseract.js (LSTM)    86.67%    84.76%           84.76%
    Scribe.js              94.52%    93.65%           93.65%

  Both reproduce exactly. The published statistic is the UNWEIGHTED MEAN OVER
  PAGES, not the pooled ratio; the two differ by more than a point here.

STEP 2 — OCRcer THROUGH THE SAME HARNESS

    setting                        pooled    mean-of-pages
    lines.column_gap_heights = 0   32.88%    33.81%
    lines.column_gap_heights = 1.5 45.84%    43.40%
    lines.column_gap_heights = 2.5 45.51%    43.13%

  Headline, on their terms: OCRcer 43.40% against Tesseract.js 84.76% and
  Scribe.js 93.65%.

WHAT THEIR METRIC COUNTS
  Per page, `correct / total` where `total` counts GROUND-TRUTH words matched
  by bounding-box overlap, punctuation ignored. That is a box-matched word
  RECALL. There is no insertion term, so the figure above says nothing at all
  about spurious output — and a change that only improves precision cannot
  move it.

STEP 3 — THIS CRATE'S OWN METRIC, SAME 29 PAGES, SAME SETTINGS (cg = 1.5)
    end-to-end   CER 109.765%  WER 270.659%  recall 44.809%  precision 15.134%  F1 22.626%

  Recall 44.8% agrees with their 43.40% to about a point, which is the second
  validation: two independent implementations of a recall-like statistic over
  different truth conversions land in the same place. Precision 15.1% is the
  half their metric cannot see.

DIAGNOSIS
  Precision 15% at recall 45% means roughly three emitted tokens for every one
  the truth has. This is not a matcher failure and better prototypes do not fix
  it: THE PIPELINE HAS NO REJECT ANYWHERE. Every component surviving the
  geometric filters is matched to its nearest class and emitted.

  A match-distance floor is the missing stage. Its value must come from the
  measured distance distributions of glyph and non-glyph components on a corpus
  containing both — not from a plausible number.

SPEED, RECORDED BUT NOT COMPARED

  These are 300 DPI full-page scans and the machine was not idle. No
