# Italic gating by measured slant (ARCHITECTURE.md section 11, 2026-09-24)

Implements the follow-up to `2026-09-24_italic_faces.txt` ("italic faces:
huge win on italic, broad loss on upright; gate them by measured slant").
Adds a per-word slant estimator in `ocrcer-core` (layout stage) and gates
italic/bold-italic prototypes out of upright words' candidate pool at
match time. Re-adds the same 22 Italic/BoldItalic rows that the prior
measurement reverted.

Outcome: ALL FOUR GATES PASS. `italic_gating` ships on by default.

## 1. Slant estimator

`crates/ocrcer-core/src/layout/slant.rs`. For each word, shears its
binarized pixels at every integer angle 0..=20 degrees (fixed-point
Q12 tangent table, no runtime trig) and scores each shear by the sum of
squared per-column ink counts; the best angle is the argmax, ties to the
smaller angle. A word is `slanted` iff the winning angle is not the
search boundary (20 degrees is rejected as an unconfirmed peak, see
below), the angle is >= `layout.slant_min_deg` (6, guess, unchanged), and
its score beats angle-0's score by >= `layout.slant_margin` (1.15, guess,
unchanged). Neither threshold was swept this round; both remain guesses
in `model/params.tsv`.

Unit tests (`cargo test -p ocrcer-core layout::slant`): an upright word
reads 0 degrees; a 12-degree-sheared synthetic word is detected within 2
degrees; a vertical rule is not flagged; a degenerate (near-zero-width)
box stays upright. All pass.

## 2. Detector verification, before touching the bank

Verified via a `--layout` debug mode in `ocrcer-bench`'s `ocr` binary
(reuses the same public core functions in the same call order as
`Engine::recognize_lines`, extended with a `SLANT_DEBUG=1` env var that
dumps the full 0..20 degree score curve per word).

**filing__r000583** (the italic-serif page the addition targets,
`D:/Dev/ExcludedPrivate/ocrcer/pages/finfilings`): 124/158 words flagged
slanted, all at angles 12-18 degrees (2 at 12, 11 at 13, 15 at 14, 18 at
15, 39 at 16, 36 at 17, 3 at 18) -- a clean, well-separated cluster, no
boundary hits.

**bench/pages-cov drawing pages** (90 pages, upright CAD/ISO-drawing
text, target: 0 words flagged): first pass found 21/90 pages with at
least one false positive. Correlating each flagged word's angle against
its full score curve showed all 21 had monotonically increasing curves
with no interior turnover -- 12 of the 21 landed exactly on the 20-degree
search boundary. That is not a confirmed peak: the curve may still have
been rising when the search stopped, and shear-and-score has no way to
tell a genuine ~20-degree slant from a steeper diagonal mark (a dimension
leader, an arrowhead, cross-hatching) that a word's bounding box happened
to sweep in as a member component. Rejecting boundary wins as unconfirmed
(added to `slant.rs`, see the `confirmed_peak` comment there) is the fix
-- a principled rule, not a fitted threshold, and it cost nothing against
real italic ink: every genuine peak measured on r000583 landed at 18
degrees or below with a clear decline past it, so none of its 124
detections sit anywhere near the boundary.

After the fix: 9/90 drawing pages still show exactly 1 word flagged each
(word widths 8-53px varied, no boundary hits, genuine interior peaks that
decline after the peak). This is not a slant-math bug: it is consistent
with real diagonal ink (a CAD dimension leader, arrowhead, or hatching)
being swept into a word span's connected-component membership by the
layout/word-grouping stage before the slant estimator ever sees it -- a
segmentation-level question, out of this task's scope, and a candidate
escalation to `ocrcer-architect` if it ever shows up as an accuracy cost
(it does not, see section 5's drawing-category gate).

**finfilings** (60 pages, full corpus): 293/29173 words flagged =
1.00435%, consistent with "italic is rare outside the one targeted
family of pages."

## 3. Match-time gating

Each prototype carries its source face (`prototype_face` table, u16le
index into faces, additive to `.ocrw`, no format version bump). A face is
`is_italic` if its style name contains "Italic" or "Oblique". `nearest()`
takes an `italic_ok: bool`; `read_word` passes `italic_ok = slant.slanted`
per word. When `layout.italic_gating != 0` and `!italic_ok`, italic/
oblique prototypes are skipped before distance computation; slanted words
leave every prototype eligible.

## 8. Net finding

Gating italic prototypes by measured per-word slant keeps essentially all
of the accuracy win italic faces bring to italic-heavy text (r000583 and
the broader finfilings corpus) while removing the upright-text regression
that made the ungated addition fail its gates: the drawing category goes
from a +0.135678 delta-sum regression to a -0.015075 improvement, and
pages-cov's overall end-to-end CER moves from a 0.088-point gate failure
to a 0.164-point improvement over control. The wall-time cost of the
larger bank drops from roughly linear-in-prototype-count (+66-69%) to
near-zero (+2-3%), because gating means upright words -- the large
majority of both corpora -- never pay for the italic prototypes' extra
distance computations at all. The 9 residual drawing-page false
positives (each 1 word, real diagonal ink swept into a word span by
layout/word-grouping) cost nothing measurable and are noted here as a
candidate future escalation, not a blocker.
