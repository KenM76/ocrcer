Photographed receipts: an accepted loss, and one corpus whose truth cannot
score CER at all
================================================================================

t=1790093454.  Both corpora under D:/Dev/ExcludedPrivate/ocrcer/pages, outside
the repository, per the standing rule that nothing downloaded from the web is

WHY THESE WERE RUN AT ALL, AND UNDER WHAT LABEL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FEASIBILITY.md section 6 condition 1 scopes this project to printed documents
and CAD drawing text.  Project rule 7 says losing to ocrs on photographs is an
accepted outcome rather than a bug to fix.  These two corpora are photographed
thermal receipts -- perspective, curl, shadow gradient, thermal dropout -- which
is closer to scene text than to a printed page.

They were run for one reason: a scope boundary that has never been measured is
an assertion.  The result below is the boundary, and it is reported as
prominently as a win because rule 8 requires it.

SROIE: A REAL LOSS, AND THE CER OVERSTATES IT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Reading the first page by hand against its truth, recognition is far better
than 136% suggests:

  want  TAN CHAY YEE                 read  tan chgy yee
  want  OJC MARKETING SDN BHD        read  OIC NARKETING SON BHO
  want  BANDAR SERI ALAM             read  BANOAR SERI AIAM
  want  TAX INVOICE                  read  TAX mvo=E

Those are wrong in a few characters each, not lost.  What destroys the score is
structure, and it is two separate things:

  1. Photographic noise becomes lines.  One band on that page came back as
     `- * *[@cg * gg - -*{*u@{gy gy@SS+- - --** - *** *- ~-- * -~ * - **`,
     which is a row of background speckle promoted to text.  Every such band is
     pure insertion, and insertions are what drive precision to 5.781% and CER
     above 100%.
  2. The reading-order defect found the same day.  Every label-and-value row
     came back value first, label second -- `; w01/2019 11:0516 AM` then `Date`
     where the truth reads `DATE : 15/01/2019 11:05:16 AM`.  A bag-of-words
     score cannot see that; CER reads the row backwards and charges for all of
     it.

So this number is a joint measurement of an out-of-domain corpus and a defect,
and it is worth re-running once the defect is fixed -- not to rescue the score,
which will still be a loss, but to find out how much of it was ours.

CORD: THE TRUTH IS NOT A PAGE TRANSCRIPT, SO THE CER MEANS NOTHING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CORD-v2's truth averages 134 characters per receipt (12,707 characters over 95
pages).  A receipt of that kind carries several times that much text.  The
`valid_line` annotation covers labelled *fields* -- menu names, counts, prices,
totals -- and not the header, the address block, the footer, or the terms.

The engine emits roughly sixteen times the truth's length, which is exactly
what an engine reading the whole page against a field-subset truth would do.
CER 1558% is therefore a measurement of the annotation's coverage, not of the
engine, and it should not be quoted.  Recall 16.532% is the only interpretable
figure and only as a lower bound: a field the engine read correctly but placed
on a different reconstructed line still counts.

Action: the CER column for CORD should be deleted rather than explained.  The
corpus stays as a word-recall spot check.

THE CAVEAT BOTH CORPORA CARRY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The converter prints it and it belongs in any report of these numbers: the
truth's WORDS are the dataset's annotation of the image, and the truth's LINE
BREAKS were reconstructed here from the word boxes by one deterministic rule
(sort by box top; break a row when a word's vertical centre leaves the row's
accumulated y-range; sort within a row by left edge).  Any CER from these
corpora is scored against that reconstruction, not against a reading of the
page, and has to say so.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT LICENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Nothing here is an argument for widening the charset, adding prototype
categories for thermal print, or building a photographic binariser.
FEASIBILITY.md section 6 is explicit that scope creep toward scene text turns a
tractable project into an intractable one.  The value of the measurement is the
boundary, not a task list.

The one thing it did produce that is in scope: the reading-order defect is not
a receipt problem.  It fires on any banded label-and-value row, which is what
an invoice and a statement are made of, and those are in domain.
