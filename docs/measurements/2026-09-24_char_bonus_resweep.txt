**drawing is the only category that regresses at every single point tested,
and it does so monotonically from the very first step above control**
(+0.030 at 3.60, rising smoothly through +0.040, +0.151, +0.407, +1.678,
+3.779). No swept value clears the drawing-category <= 0 gate except the
control itself, where the delta is 0 by construction. Six other tested
increments (3.60 through 4.50) all pass the pages-cov end-to-end gate and
improve five of the six other categories, but every one of them fails the
joint gate on drawing alone.

This is not noise: the six drawing deltas are smooth and monotone across
seven points spanning 3.44 to 7.5, and it is the mechanism rule 6 and the
task brief both name in advance -- a larger char_bonus rewards a merged,
wide-edge reading over two-or-more separate correct characters (the exact
arithmetic the letter autopsy traced for "Brookfield"'s r+o and "factors,"'s
f+a). Drawing-category pages are CAD/ISO text: short runs of digits,
tolerances and part-like tokens where that is precisely the failure mode
identifier-shaped text is most exposed to, and where there is little running
English text or lexicon coverage to outweigh it. currency, invoice, prose,
statement and twins all *improve* through 4.50 because they carry enough
ordinary prose/number-with-units text for the decoder-loss fix (two good
single-character matches beating one merged one) to dominate; drawing does
not, and pays the identifier-rewrite cost that fix also carries.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. Disposition
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Nothing passes.** `decode.char_bonus` stays at 3.44 in both
`crates/ocrcer-core/src/params.rs` and `model/params.tsv`. No rebuild, no
`.ocrw` change. This measurement file is the only artifact from this
session.

The letter autopsy's back-solved ~5.9 was explicitly a two-case estimate on
one page, flagged in its own text as needing a full-corpus check before
being trusted ("this is a guess against a sample of 2 decoder-stage losses
on one page"). That check is now done: 5.9 fails both the pages-cov
end-to-end gate and the drawing-category gate badly (+1.678), and the whole
region above char_bonus=3.44 that improves prose-like categories costs the
drawing category monotonically, with no crossover found down to the
smallest increment tried (+0.16). The 4 decoder-stage losses the autopsy
traced are real, but the fix that would rescue them on the italic filing
page is not free elsewhere in the corpus, and the corpus-wide, dual-gate
measurement this task calls for is exactly what catches that trade before it
ships.

Carried forward: if `char_bonus` is revisited again, the next lever is not
a further increase but something that discriminates by context -- for
example, a segmentation- or identifier-shape-aware adjustment to the merge
penalty that does not treat every wide edge alike -- rather than a single
global scalar that necessarily trades prose accuracy against drawing-text
identifier safety in the same direction every time it moves. That is an
`ocrcer-architect` scope question, not a parameter value, and is not
implemented here.
