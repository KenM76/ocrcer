  tables (`ocrcer-glyphs`) or a fix to the segmentation-vs-classification
  split in table rows (`ocrcer-runtime`) — not another decoder-weight or
  scale retune, which has now been tried and shown not to move this cluster
  across three separate measurements.
- **This is the pre-registered final re-measure for this cycle** ("chunk 15
  mode 1 now gets its one further re-measure... If that also fails, the
  next net step is branch A"). It fails. Per the 2026-09-27 "chunk 15 step 2
  fails again" entry's own decision, the next net step (if pursued) is
  branch A: training data covering dense small-font digit rows, under a new
  entry — not a further re-measure under the current segmentation and
  decoder configuration.
- The `")"->"h"` and `"."->"e"` pairs newly visible in this full 80-row
